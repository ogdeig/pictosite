Good to know

Does the artwork need a QR code?

No. Pic To Link is designed to identify the appearance of the artwork itself. The early preview uses basic fingerprints; robust matching on printed or distorted images remains under development.

Do I have to sign in to scan?

No. Scanning is free and doesn't require an account.

Can I change a linked website later?

Yes. Open My Toolbox and edit the link. Editing an existing registration never uses another image allowance.

Who can register a design?

Only someone who has permission to use the artwork and connect the destination. A public launch will include verification, content review, and a dispute process.
